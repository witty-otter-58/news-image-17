# 17 November 2026 in one image

One newspaper front page that sums up Tuesday 17 November 2026, built for
make.fun's [news-image-20261117](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117)
bounty. The picture is `entry.png` (1024x1024); `main.ts` copies it to the
grader's output folder. The page itself was laid out in `design/index.html`
and rendered to a screenshot with headless Chromium.

## The stories on the page

- **Lead, Middle East:** US strikes Iran after drones hit Bahrain base —
  Iranian drones targeted a US radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain; US
  warplanes hit a missile site on Qeshm Island in reply; a tanker burned
  off Oman and 24 seafarers were evacuated; Houthi drones struck a Saudi
  pipeline.
- **Ukraine:** Kerch Bridge hit as Kyiv bridges burn — Ukrainian missiles
  brought down part of the Crimean Bridge; Russia answered with 306 drones
  and missiles on Kyiv's bridges; Zelenskyy asked allies for more Patriots
  and F-16s.
- **Gaza:** Winter rains deepen Gaza's shelter crisis — the UN warned
  floods are wrecking makeshift shelters as food runs low; Britain,
  Australia, Canada and Italy urged Israel to open crossings for aid.
- **Health:** US loses measles elimination status — measles is endemic in
  the US again, the first time since 2000.
- **Markets:** Wall Street falls as Nvidia turmoil deepens — AI stocks
  slid; ex-chief Jensen Huang resigned amid a spying scandal and the board
  will not bring him back, despite record quarterly revenue.
- **Also today:** COP31 talks in Antalya entered a decisive phase;
  Israeli airstrikes and shelling hit southern Lebanon; Tiger Woods
  announced his return in the Bahamas; Depardieu admitted a sex-assault
  charge in Paris.

Every story on the page happened or was first reported on 17 November 2026.
